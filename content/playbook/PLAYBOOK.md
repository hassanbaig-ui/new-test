## 2. What the competitors do

**Al Qaim Autos** (typical 61.5K, best 155K) — the reach machine
- Every reel is a tip for the viewer: "Your AC is not cooling because…", "How to increase AC cooling".
- 10–27 seconds, one idea.
- Bold hook text on screen from frame 1.
- Gross or satisfying close-up: black sludge in a palm, choked condenser fins.
- Hands-only POV. No face, no shop selling in the video.
- English "you" captions + viral hashtags → reaches beyond Pakistan.
- Weakness: no face, no trust, no local leads.

**Yaseen Car AC** (285K followers, typical 9.5K, best 1.8M) — the closest to FineCool
- Red/pink Urdu text bar on screen the whole reel (all 10 reels).
- Emotion and story hooks win: "تکلیف ہوتی ہے ایسے نقصان دیکھ کر" (1.8M), "ہفتہ پہلے یہ گاڑی خریدی ہے" (10K), question "سردیوں میں کیا کام کریں" (23K).
- Talks to camera like a friend, hands moving, mid-sentence at frame 1.
- Crowd and customer in frame (social proof).
- Already posting winter topics.
- Weakness: 4 reels a day dilutes reach, no result shown, long reels (96–180s) stall.

**Auto Doctor** (best 1.2M) — 11–13 second DIY tricks, "How to…" captions, zero selling.

**Afaaq, Abdullah** (typical 200–450) — listing captions + phone number. This is what FineCool must stop doing.

## 4. Hook formulas

Standard hooks (reliable, 5–15K):
1. **Problem + car**: "Is [car] ka AC thanda nahi kar raha tha…"
2. **Payoff first**: "Pehle [18] degree… ab dekhein"
3. **DIY check**: "[Gas kam hai?] 10 second mein check karo"
4. **Common mistake**: "Ye ghalti har doosra driver karta hai"
5. **Question**: "Aap ki gari ka AC kitne degree pe hai?"

Top-level hooks (higher ceiling, Yaseen/Al Qaim style):
1. **Damage + emotion**: "Ye haal dekh ke takleef hui…" (Yaseen 1.8M)
2. **Others failed**: "3 workshop ghoom ke aaya, masla sirf [500] ka nikla"
3. **New-car story**: "Hafta pehle nayi gari li, aur AC ne jawab de diya"
4. **Gross reveal**: close-up of sludge/dirt + "AC thanda nahi? 90% wajah ye hai 🤮" (Al Qaim 155K)
5. **Curiosity contrast**: "Mehran ka AC Land Cruiser se zyada thanda?"
6. **Money shock**: "Mistri ne 40 hazaar bola, kharcha [2 hazaar] aaya"
7. **Myth bust**: "Gas bharwane se AC theek nahi hota. Asal masla ye hai"

Rules: the hook is the first sentence; no salaam, no intro; screen text = 5–7 Urdu words, red bar, on screen the whole reel.

| Time | Step | What |

Caption: line 1 = question or hook; one line of story; then "Fine Cool Car AC · Multan · Full warranty" + hashtags. Phone number goes in the pinned comment.

## 6. Weekly mix

Mon Mehran/Alto series · Tue dirty-part tip · Wed real job + payoff · Thu 12s DIY check · Fri curiosity car · Sat teaching/myth · Sun customer story. All car AC, Sherry on camera.

## 7. Seasonal calendar (Multan)

- Apr–Sep: cooling, compressor, gas, condenser, "AC thanda nahi".
- Oct–Nov: post-summer service, leaks found after summer, "service now" offers.
- Dec–Feb: heater not working, AC smell/mould, foggy windscreen, compressor noise, "service before summer" pre-booking.
- Mar: pre-summer rush, "summer se pehle AC check".

## 8. Never

Duplicates · plain "installation with warranty" openers · phone number as caption line 1 · off-topic trends · fake reviews · unverified prices or specs · bots posting in Facebook groups.
