# Instant Replies, Group Playbook and Funnel

Business facts in `[brackets]` must be filled from `business.md` before use.

## 1. Instant replies (comments and DMs)

Set these up once as **saved replies** and **automated responses** in Meta Business Suite (Facebook + Instagram) and **quick replies** in WhatsApp Business. Free and allowed by Meta.

| Trigger (keyword or question) | Reply (Roman Urdu) |
|---|---|
| price / kitne ka / rate | "Shukriya! Rate gari aur masle pe depend karta hai. Apni gari ka model aur masla WhatsApp karein: [0300-0873036]. Check karke exact bata dein ge." |
| location / kahan / address | "📍 Fine Cool Car AC, [address], Multan. Google Maps: [link]. Timing: [timing]." |
| warranty | "Har kaam full warranty ke saath: [warranty terms]." |
| kitna time | "Zyada tar kaam [same day] ho jata hai. Pehle WhatsApp pe gari ka model bhej dein." |
| other city | "Hum Multan mein hain. [Courier parts / video guidance: yes/no]. WhatsApp karein: [number]." |
| complaint | "Maazrat chahte hain. Please WhatsApp pe gari number aur masla bhejein, Sherry bhai khud dekhein ge: [number]." |
| praise | "Bohat shukriya! ❤️ Apni gari ka AC kitne degree pe hai? 😄" |

Comment rule: reply to every comment in the first hour. A reply with a question gets a second comment, which pushes the reel further.

Upgrade later (paid, Meta-approved): **ManyChat** keyword automation. Example: reel caption says "Comment AC", ManyChat sends the WhatsApp link in DM automatically.

## 2. Facebook group

**Name:** Multan Car Owners – AC & Car Care Help
**Join questions:** 1) Which car? 2) Which area of Multan? 3) WhatsApp number (optional, for offers).
**Rule:** a human posts and replies. Facebook removed group posting through its API in 2024, and bots or scrapers that post in groups break Facebook's rules and can get the page banned. Claude drafts; the manager posts.

Weekly group calendar:

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Share Mehran Monday reel + "Aap ki Mehran ka AC kaisa hai?" |
| Tue | Problem poll: "Sab se zyada kya masla aata hai? 1) Cooling kam 2) Trip 3) Smell 4) Noise" |
| Wed | Reel share + member question answered in detail |
| Thu | Quick tip (text + photo) |
| Fri | "Ask Sherry" thread: post your AC problem, Sherry answers |
| Sat | Customer before/after photo (with permission) |
| Sun | Members-only offer: "[Free AC check this week for group members]" |

## 3. Funnel: viewer → group → page → WhatsApp → job

1. **Reel** (reach): every caption ends with the question; pinned comment holds WhatsApp + group link.
2. **Group** (trust): members get answers and offers; each answer links the matching reel.
3. **Page follow**: pin a post in the group "Roz nayi video: [page link]".
4. **WhatsApp** (lead): every reply routes to WhatsApp; greeting message asks car model + problem + area.
5. **Job**: Sherry asks "Video kahan dekhi?" and the manager logs it.
6. **Back to content**: happy customer → tomorrow's customer-story reel.
7. **WhatsApp channel/broadcast** (repeat): service reminders in March, winter offers in December.

## 4. YouTube

Upload every reel as a Short (same file). Once a week, a long video (8–12 min) stitched from the week's jobs: "Ek hafta FineCool workshop mein" or "Mehran AC ke 5 masle aur hal". Long videos and a "Subscribe" line at the payoff turn Shorts viewers into subscribers.
