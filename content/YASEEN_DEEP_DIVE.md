## What actually makes his reels work

1. **A pink Urdu headline sticker that stays on screen for the whole reel.** All 10 have one. Even with the sound off, viewers know what the video is about. FineCool rarely does this.
2. **Story or emotion beats selling.** His 1.8M hook is a *feeling* ("it hurts to see this damage"), shot while sitting with the customer next to a destroyed compressor, with a crowd watching. His story hook ("bought this car a week ago") got 10K. His self-promo ("rush even in winter", "share karein", a date sticker) gets 3–6K.
3. **He talks to the camera like he's talking to a friend.** Standing, hands moving, mid-sentence from frame 1. No intro, no salaam.
4. **Crowd and customer in the frame** add social proof and real drama.
5. **Questions to the audience** ("what should we do in winter?") turn into comments, and comments turn into reach (23K).

## His weaknesses (your opening)

- **285K followers but a median of about 9.5K.** Most of his reels reach only a few percent of his followers. Volume (3–4 a day) is diluting him.
- **No payoff shot.** No temperature reading, no before/after. His reels just end.
- **Long reels with no structure** (96–180s) sit at 5–9K.
- **Weak hooks on half his reels** (a date sticker, "share karein", no text at all).
- **No series.** Every reel is a one-off.
