## 2. What the winners do that you don't

### Al Qaim Autos: "the tip machine" (median 61.5K)
- **Every reel is a tip for the viewer, not an ad for the shop:** *"How to increase AC cooling"*, *"Your AC is not cooling because of dirty choked condenser"*, *"Change the $10 filter for better cooling and compressor life"*.
- **10–27 seconds.** One idea per reel, so people finish it and Facebook pushes it.
- **Bold hook text on screen from frame 1** (*"Cooling coil Flushing 🤮"*).
- **A gross or satisfying visual:** black sludge in the palm, a choked condenser. People can't scroll past it.
- Hands-only POV. No intro, no face, no phone number in the video.
- English captions with "you" + viral hashtags. That pulls a foreign audience too.

### Auto Doctor: "the 12-second trick" (1.2M)
- **11–13 seconds, every time.** *"AC gas check"*: press the valve, see the result. A trick viewers can try on their own car.
- Captions are a "How to …" question. Zero selling.

### Yaseen Car AC: "the closest to you" (1.8M)
- Same model as you (owner, workshop, real jobs). His 1.8M was a **Mira AC problem**, a mass-market car, same as your Mehran hit.
- **A crowd standing around the job** (social proof), plus a **picture-in-picture close-up** of the faulty part so you see the problem and his face together.
- Captions **ask a question or give advice**, and those beat his own sales captions:
  - *"سردیوں میں کیا کیا کام کر سکتے ہیں کمنٹ میں بتائیں"* → **23K** (his best recent reel)
  - *"گاڑی کسی اچھے بندے سے دیکھ کر لیں"* → 10K
  - *"جس نے بھی گارنٹی کے ساتھ کام کروانا ہے رابطہ کریں"* (sales) → 3–5K
- He's already **pivoting to winter topics**. AC demand drops in Nov–Feb, so plan for that now.

### Afaaq and Abdullah: what *not* to do (median 200–450)
- Captions are a service listing + phone number (*"Kia Sportage AC leaking problem solution… best shop… contact number…"*). That's the same pattern as your *"X full ac installation with full warranty…"*, and they get a few hundred views.
- Abdullah posts 4+ reels a day of brakes, tyres and AC mixed together. Volume without a hook does nothing.

## 3. The formula, ranked by evidence

| Signal | Winners | Losers (incl. most FineCool reels) |

| Length | 10–30s (Al Qaim, Auto Doctor) | 45–95s |
| Hook | On-screen text + gross/satisfying shot at 0s | Person standing, no text |
| Angle | "**Your** AC isn't cooling because…" | "**We** installed AC with warranty" |
| Caption line 1 | How-to / why / question | Car model + "full warranty" + phone |
| Topic | Mass car (Mehran/Mira/Alto) or DIY trick | Installation of the day |
| CTA | Comment question | "Rabta karein" / phone number |

## 4. Your playbook: steal, then beat

**Steal from Al Qaim (2 reels/week):** 15–25s "why your AC isn't cooling" tips. Hands-only POV + Urdu hook text. Ideas: dirty condenser, choked cabin filter, sludge from a seized compressor, a weak magnetic clutch, ice on the pipe.

**Steal from Auto Doctor (1–2/week):** a 12-second DIY check. *"Gas kam hai ya nahi, 10 second mein check karo."* No selling.

**Steal from Yaseen (3/week):** your current owner-on-camera jobs, plus a crowd/customer in frame, a picture-in-picture close-up of the faulty part, and a question caption.

**Beat them all (your edge):** none of them shows the **vent thermometer payoff**. You have it. End every reel on "**8°C**" in big text. And none of them do **Mehran / Alto / Mira series**; you already own the 1.3M Mehran reel. Make it a weekly series: *"Mehran Monday."*

**Winter plan (start Nov):** heater not working, AC smell, demister/fog, "service your AC before summer" discount, compressor-noise diagnosis.
