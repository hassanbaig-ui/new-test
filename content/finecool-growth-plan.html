The one-minute summary

Where we are
  • 108 reels posted, 23 Jun to 6 Oct 2026.
  • Typical reel went from about 400 views (June–July) to about 8,000 (September). The switch to Sherry on camera doing real jobs caused this.
  • Hits: 1.3M (modified Mehran), 292K (1993 Land Cruiser), 246K (Alto compressor bearing), 157K (Mehran repair).
  • Since 25 Sep the typical reel dropped to about 5,400.
Why it's stuck
  • Most reels look and read the same: "X full AC installation with full warranty".
  • No hook in the first 2 seconds and no Urdu text on screen.
  • Reels are too long (70–95s) for one idea.
  • No ending payoff (the cold temperature reading).
  • 7 duplicate reels were posted in one minute on 24 Sep.
The plan in one line: keep Sherry on camera, but turn every reel into a short story with a hook, Urdu text on screen, and the thermometer at the end. Post one strong reel a day on Facebook, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, and track calls, not just views.
Sherry bhai ke liye (Roman Urdu)

Sherry bhai, aap ki videos chal rahi hain. June mein 400 views thay, September mein 8,000. Aap ka chehra aur aap ka kaam hi brand hai. Bas 4 cheezein badalni hain:

  1. Pehle 2 second mein masla batayein. Salaam ya intro nahi, seedha: "Is Mehran ka AC 3 workshop se theek nahi hua…"
  2. Video chhoti rakhein: 30 se 45 second.
  3. Aakhir mein thermometer dikhayein: "Pehle 18 degree, ab 8 degree."
  4. Customer ko saath khara karein aur us se 1 line bulwayein.

Baaki sab (caption, posting, TikTok, group) media manager sambhalega.

Who does what

Sherry Owner and video hero

About 20 minutes a day, on camera.

  • Pick the most interesting car of the day (problem car, old car, Mehran/Alto, unusual damage).
  • Record the hook line first, then the problem, the fix and the result.
  • Show the faulty part close to the camera.
  • End every job with the vent thermometer shot.
  • Ask the customer for one reaction line ("Pehle garmi se bura haal tha…").
  • Answer the top 5 comments with a short voice note or reply (builds trust).

Media manager Social media and marketing

About 1.5 hours a day.

  • Plan the day's video and send Sherry the hook line in the morning.
  • Edit: cut to 30–45s, add the red Urdu text bar, add the temperature text at the end.
  • Write the caption (question first) and pin the phone number as the first comment.
  • Post to Facebook, then TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
  • Run the Facebook group and WhatsApp channel.
  • Log views, calls and jobs every day. Run the weekly review with Sherry.

The FineCool video formula

Every reel follows these 5 steps. 30–45 seconds total.

0–2s
Hook. Sherry mid-sentence, car in frame. Red Urdu text bar on screen with the problem in 5–7 words.

Stakes. Why it matters: "Customer 40 hazaar compressor pe laga chuka tha…"
8–25s
Fix. Close-up of the faulty part, then the repair. One idea only.
25–32s
Payoff. Thermometer in the vent. Big text: 18° → 8°. Customer reaction.
32–36s
Question. "Aap ki gari ka AC kitne degree pe hai? Comment karein."

Rules

Sherry Filming rules

  1. Start talking in the first second. No salaam, no intro.
  2. Phone vertical, straight, at chest height. Never tilted.
  3. Car and face both in frame.
  4. Show the faulty part close to the lens.
  5. Always film the thermometer before and after.
  6. One car, one problem, one video.
  7. Talk like talking to a friend: energy and hands.

Manager Posting rules

  1. Red Urdu text bar on screen the whole reel.
  2. 30–45s (tips 15–25s). Cut every pause.
  3. Caption line 1 is a question. Phone number goes in the pinned comment.
  4. One reel a day. Never post duplicates.
  5. Post the same reel to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
  6. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  7. Only car AC and car care. No memes, no unrelated trends.
