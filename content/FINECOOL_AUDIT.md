## 1. The verdict in one line

You don't have a reach problem; you have a **sameness problem**. Since mid-August the algorithm gives every reel an 8K test audience. Reels that look and read like the last 50 ("X full ac installation with full warranty") die there. The 6 reels that went big each had something the others didn't: **a curiosity car, one satisfying mechanical moment, or a face-to-camera reaction in the first second.**

## 3. What you're missing (ranked by impact)

1. **No hook in the first 2 seconds.** Typical reels open on a static medium shot: you standing or sitting, sometimes **a tilted/sideways camera** (seen in a 4.6K reel), with no on-screen text. A scroller can't tell what the problem is. Your hits open with you *mid-sentence, mid-gesture, car in frame*.
   → **Fix:** start every reel with the **problem** on screen as large Urdu text plus your line: *"Is Mehran ka AC 3 workshop se theek nahi hua…"*. No salaam, no intro.

2. **Captions are a service listing, not a story.** About 70 captions follow one copy-paste formula: *"[Car] full ac installation with full warranty FineCoolCarAc Multan 0300…"*. On Facebook the caption is your second hook, and right now it reads like an ad, which Facebook distributes less.
   → **Fix:** line 1 = curiosity or problem (*"Compressor ki bearing ne 2 lakh ka nuksaan bacha liya"*). Number and hashtags go at the bottom only.

3. **The same reel every day.** "Installation + warranty" is what *you* sell, not what *viewers* want to watch. Most viewers don't have a broken AC today; they watch for curiosity, a satisfying fix, or a lesson. You've posted dozens of near-identical installs.
   → **Fix:** rotate 4 formats (section 4) and never post 2 of the same format back to back.

4. **The spam event on 23–24 Sep.** Seven identical 30-second Rivo clips posted in the same minute (543–864 views each, 10× below your baseline) plus an 18-minute video. Since then the median is 5.4K vs 8.7K before. That's a correlation, not proof, but duplicate bursts are a known distribution penalty.
   → **Fix:** never post duplicates. Max 1–2 reels/day. If you have multiple clips of one job, combine them into one reel or space them out as different angles (problem / fix / result).

5. **No payoff shot.** Hits end with a result. Most reels just stop. You have the best payoff in the niche: **the vent thermometer reading** ("8.7°C") — and it's barely used.
   → **Fix:** every reel ends (or opens) on the thermometer close-up with the number as text.

6. **Too long for the content.** Most reels run 70–95s while saying one thing. The short hits (31s, 23s) did 246K and 24K. Long reels only work when you're *explaining* (Cultus 2:58 = 45K).
   → **Fix:** a single fix = 25–40s. An explanation reel = 90s+ only if you talk the whole time.

7. **Few comment triggers.** You used *"Aap ki gari kitni cooling karti hai, comment mein batain"* once. That's the right idea; comments push distribution.
   → **Fix:** one forced-choice question per reel (*"Mehran ya Alto, kis ka AC zyada thanda?"*).

8. **Posting time is never tested.** Almost everything goes out at 12–1pm PKT. Pakistani Facebook peaks around 8–11pm.
   → **Test:** post 10 reels at 9pm PKT and compare medians.

## 4. The 4-format rotation (replace the daily "installation" post)

| Format | Share | Example | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Curiosity car** | 25% | Modified, old, rare or truck/van ("1986 Corolla ka AC 2026 mein") | 45–90s |
| **B. One satisfying fix** | 35% | Bearing, ice on pipe, leak found with dye. Close-up hands, 1 problem. | 25–40s |
| **C. Teach / myth-bust** | 25% | "Local vs genuine cooling coil", "AC trip kyun hota hai", "Gas bharwane se pehle ye check karo" | 45–90s |
| **D. Proof / customer** | 15% | Customer reacts to the temperature, "3 workshop ke baad yahan theek hua" | 20–40s |

## 5. Script template (owner-on-camera, Roman Urdu)

```
[0–2s]  HOOK  On-screen text = the problem in 5 words. You, mid-sentence, car in frame.
        "Is Alto ka AC chalte chalte band ho jata tha…"
[2–8s]  STAKES  Why it matters / what others did wrong.
        "…3 mistri compressor badalne ka keh chuke the. 40 hazaar ka kharcha."
[8–25s] FIX  Close-ups of the actual fault and fix. One idea, no list of services.
        "Asal masla compressor nahi, bearing thi. Dekhain…"
[25–32s] PAYOFF  Thermometer close-up + number as text.
        "Ab vent pe 7 degree. Wohi compressor, 40 hazaar bach gaye."
[32–36s] CTA  One question, not a sales pitch.
        "Aap ki gari ka AC kitne degree pe hai? Comment karo."
Caption line 1 = hook sentence. Number + hashtags at the bottom.
```

## 6. Five reels to shoot this week

1. **"Mehran ka AC Land Cruiser jitna thanda?"** (A): Mehran vs Land Cruiser vent temperature, side by side.
2. **"Ye bearing 500 ki hai, mistri 40 hazaar ka compressor bolta hai"** (B): bearing fault close-up, 30s.
3. **"Local freezer vs genuine: andar se kaat ke dekho"** (C): cut both open on camera.
4. **"AC gas har saal kyun khatam hoti hai?"** (C): leak dye test, 3 common leak points.
5. **"Customer ka reaction: 3 workshop ke baad"** (D): a real customer feels the vent, temperature on screen.

## 7. Track what pays, not just views

Your customers are in Multan; the 1.3M views were mostly the rest of Pakistan. Ask every caller *"video kahan dekhi?"* and log calls per week next to each reel. Views build the brand; **calls per reel** tell you which format makes money. Formats B and D usually win on calls even when A wins on views.
